# CSIRT.global

> A volunteer-led, not-for-profit foundation registered in the Netherlands. We
> scan the public internet for known vulnerabilities and exposed credentials,
> identify the owners of the affected systems, and notify them. Every case we
> open is published.

Most people reading this arrived from an unexpected email saying a system they
own is vulnerable, and want to know whether it is genuine. The organisation
record below is verifiable on registers that are not us, and
{{ "/notified/" | absURL }} is the page written for that question.

Two facts that settle most of it: notifications come from an address ending in
`@csirt.global`, and CSIRT.global never asks for payment, credentials, or access
to a system.

CSIRT.global is a sister organisation of the Dutch Institute for Vulnerability
Disclosure (DIVD). International CSIRT activities moved here in 2022.
