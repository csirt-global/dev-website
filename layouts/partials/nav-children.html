{{- /* The child pages of one nav item, as a slice of {url, label, key}. Derived from the section's own pages when the item opts in with `expand: true`, so adding a page under /what-we-do/ puts it in the menu without anyone editing the nav. Sections that grow without limit (Cases, News) do not opt in: a menu is not a place to paginate. data/nav.yaml can also add entries that are not child pages, via its own `children:` list. A page with `hidden: true` in its front matter is left out: that is how the donate page stays out of the menu until its settings are filled in. Everything resolves through href.html, so a menu entry never points at a translation that does not exist. */ -}} {{- $item := .item -}} {{- $out := slice -}} {{- if $item.expand -}} {{- with site.GetPage $item.url -}} {{- range where .Pages "Params.hidden" "!=" true -}} {{- $out = $out | append (dict "url" .RelPermalink "label" .Title "desc" (or .Params.summary "")) -}} {{- end -}} {{- end -}} {{- end -}} {{- range $item.children -}} {{- $c := . -}} {{- $label := "" -}} {{- with site.GetPage $c.url }}{{ $label = .Title }}{{ end -}} {{- with $c.key }}{{ $label = i18n . }}{{ end -}} {{- $out = $out | append (dict "url" (partial "href.html" $c.url) "label" $label "desc" "") -}} {{- end -}} {{- return $out -}}
