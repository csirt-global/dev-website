Project Global NGO's (pgngo) is an initiative dedicated to identifying and addressing unpatched
security vulnerabilities within NGO's worldwide. These organisations are often threatened by cyber
threats even though they often don't have the resources to defend themselves. We aim to help these
organisations by finding vulnerabilities and alerting the organisations, as well as giving them
advice on how to resolve the vulnerability.

## The Project

### Why we help NGOs

NGO's are important to society and do a lot of good work. We would like to help them take some of the
worry about cyber threats away. We are a volunteer-led, not-for-profit organisation ourselves, we
just want to help with no alternative motive.

### What we do

We scan any subdomain we can find with tools like Nuclei. Any vulnerabilities we find with those
tools are manually confirmed by our volunteers before we notify the organisation in question to
prevent unnecessary alarm.

If you want to be scanned more often, we would be happy to help out. You can
[email us](mailto:inquiries@csirt.global?subject=Project%20Global%20NGOs) (please use your work email
for this, so we can tell you actually belong to this organisation) and tell us which domains you
would want us to scan and what email address to send a report to.

### Why we do this

Our main priority is to make the digital world a safer place, but this project also aims to help
people gain more experience in cyber security. Both manually confirming and communicating with people
from the NGO's themselves teaches valuable skills.
